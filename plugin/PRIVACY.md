# Privacy and terms

Rapier needs no account signup and uses no analytics or advertising. Your files stay on your device until you save, share, back up or sync them. Sync uses your bucket, encrypted with a key that never leaves your devices. You keep or delete local files; Delete forever removes notes and their history.

The Android editor has no Internet permission. It records audio only when you press Record, into your notes. Google Play handles purchases and optional plugin downloads under its policy.

Sharing a document through the hosted agent door (mcp.rapier.website) sends the content you choose there for shared reading and editing. Its private workspace keeps the document, retained edit history, inspected passages, presence, selections and tool records. Authorized connections and secret document references control workspace access. Active use renews workspace expiry to 30 days; unchanged sync does not. Expired workspaces are cleared on their next request or deletion alarm. In the editor, you can delete the workspace or disconnect agents from that document. The door keeps creation and registration budget records for one hour and tool invocation budget records for one minute. Your assistant's host handles the conversation under its policy.

Essential cookies remember the browser that approved connected apps for up to 90 days, guard consent, and authorize paired editors for up to 24 hours. OAuth access tokens expire after 15 minutes. Refresh grants and client registrations expire after 90 days without renewal. You can revoke a connected app at Connected apps. Revoking a connection preserves your hosted documents; delete their workspaces to remove them.

Hosted exports keep an unchanged file copy. A download link lasts up to 24 hours within the workspace's lifetime, and is revoked when you delete the workspace or disconnect agents. Expired copies are cleared on the next workspace update or deletion.

Cloudflare serves the page and door and sees what web requests show.
