# Changelog

## 1.2.55 (2026-11-04)

- The JPEG XL button in Settings opens the converter, on the web and in the apps.
- Make animated JPEG XL from pictures, GIFs and videos in the converter's animated tab.
- REORDER in the headings list moves sections by dragging.
- The scroll circle follows the page smoothly.
- Save shows a tick once the file is saved.
- A list holding indented code opens formatted.
- A quote card's picture carries only what the card shows.
- Fixed: the watercolor Eraser clears all the paint under it.

## 1.2.54 (2026-11-02)

- Send feedback and bug reports from Settings.
- Save a 3D view or panorama as JPEG XL.

## 1.2.53 (2026-10-31)

- Pictures use rapier-jxl 8.0.1: uploads encode 25-34% faster on one thread.

## 1.2.52 (2026-10-31)

- Pictures use rapier-jxl 8.0, built into the page: they save and open offline with nothing more to download.
- Imported notes keep every character, whatever their text encoding.
- Fixed: in an embedded editor, an oddly written picture address could load a remote picture without asking.
- The JPEG XL converter page shows how to use rapier-jxl 8.0 in your own site.

## 1.2.51 (2026-10-28)

- New Block button in the editing toolbar: move, space, copy, cut or delete the block you're in.
- The editing toolbar is reordered. Will and expanding sections are in the + menu.

## 1.2.50 (2026-10-28)

- Fixed: a tap or drag on a block's first word places the caret or selects, instead of opening spacing.
- Block spacing is on the bar you get when you hold a block.
- Diagrams draw in Rapier's own style.
