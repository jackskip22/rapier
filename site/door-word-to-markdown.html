Word to Markdown

Convert Word documents to Markdown ready for AI tools and LLMs: headings, lists, tables, links and pictures, the pictures inside the .md file. Rapier reads the Word document on your device, offline: nothing is uploaded.

Also: Markdown to Word · PDF to Markdown · Markdown converter · Compare text and documents

How to convert Word to Markdown

  1. Choose or drop .docx files. Convert to is on Markdown.
  2. Download each .md file, with the pictures inside it.
  3. Or tap Edit to open it in Rapier as an editable Markdown page.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Rapier is free and open source, with no account, ads or tracking.

Is my document uploaded?

No. Rapier works in your browser. Local editing uploads nothing.

Does it keep tables and pictures?

Yes. Tables become Markdown tables, or HTML where cells are merged, and pictures go inside the .md file.

Can I also convert a PDF?

Yes. Drop a PDF too. PDF reading is a free plug-in, downloaded once.
