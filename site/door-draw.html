Free drawing app online

Rapier Draw opens a canvas in your browser. Draw with your finger on a phone, or with a mouse or a pen. Select a shape to move, resize, label or restyle it, or attach an arrow that moves with it. Drawings are SVG: sharp at any size and editable later.

Questions

Can I draw on my phone?

Yes. Rapier is made for phones first: draw with your finger, and pinch to zoom.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Draw runs in your browser. There is also an Android app on Google Play.

Where does my drawing go?

Press Done and it goes into your document as an editable SVG. Save the document as one Markdown file, pictures inside.

Is it free?

Yes. Rapier is free and open source, with no account, ads or tracking.
