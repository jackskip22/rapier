PDF to Markdown

Convert PDF files to Markdown ready for AI tools and LLMs, with their pictures. Rapier reads the PDF on your device, offline: nothing is uploaded, so a private PDF never reaches a server. Check the reading order, or tap Edit to fix it in Rapier.

How to convert PDF to Markdown

  1. Choose or drop .pdf files. Convert to is on Markdown.
  2. The first time, Rapier downloads its free PDF reader once.
  3. Download each .md file, or tap Edit to check the reading order in Rapier.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Rapier is free and open source, with no account, ads or tracking.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF reader runs in your browser.

Does it keep the pictures?

Yes. Pictures go inside the Markdown file.

Does a PDF made in Rapier come back exactly?

Yes. A PDF Rapier exports carries its Markdown, and converting it gives back the same file.
