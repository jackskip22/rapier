formats

What goes in, what comes out, and what each entry weighs.

Goes inDepth and colorComes outSize

goes in

  • Pixels

    A picture is width x height pixels of straight RGBA samples.

  • DecodedImage

    header is the whole file's image header, as decode parsed it: header()'s fields for the same file, plus icc, the file's own profile (header() reads no profile and may read only the file's first bytes).

  • CanvasSurface

    A 2D, WebGL or WebGPU canvas, or an OffscreenCanvas, read at its size.

  • BitmapSurface

    An ImageBitmap, read at its size.

  • FrameSurface

    A WebCodecs VideoFrame (a camera, a screen capture, a decoded video): read at its display size, converted by the browser from its own color space to sRGB, or to readAs.

  • VideoSurface

    A <video> (playing, or paused on a frame): read as the frame it shows, as a VideoFrame of it is.

  • Blob

    An image file the browser can decode, read in sRGB or its own space.

  • RowSource

    A picture given a band of rows at a time, so it is never whole in memory: rows(y, count) returns rows y to y + count - 1 as {data} (width x count RGBA samples, described by the source) or a canvas, bitmap or video frame of width x count (8-bit, in the source's colorSpace: sRGB or Display P3).

  • TextureInput

    A GPU texture read from its own device, at the encoder's size.

depth, color and HDR

  • samples Uint8Array | Uint8ClampedArray | Uint16Array | Float16Samples | Float32Array

    The typed arrays a picture's samples may be in: 8 or 16 bit integers, half or full floats.

  • bitDepth 8 | 10 | 12 | 16 | 32 | undefined

    Integer code-value depth: 8 for bytes. 10, 12, or 16 for Uint16Array (default 16). Floating-point depth is fixed by the sample format: 16 or 32.

  • sampleFormat SampleFormat | undefined

    Inferred as uint for integer arrays or float32 for Float32Array. float16 uses raw IEEE binary16 words in Uint16Array.

  • transferFunction TransferFunction | undefined

    Declares the samples' existing transfer function. Defaults to srgb for integers, linear for floats. No color conversion.

  • colorSpace ColorSpace | undefined

    Source color space, declared without conversion. Default 'srgb'.

  • intensityTarget number | undefined

    Positive peak luminance in nits, stored as binary16. Defaults to 10000 for PQ, 1000 for HLG, otherwise 255.

  • alphaPremultiplied boolean | undefined

    Source RGB is premultiplied by alpha. Default false. Values are never unpremultiplied.

  • icc Uint8Array | undefined

    Original defined three-component ICC.1 profile or GRAY, kept unchanged, without color conversion. Mutually exclusive with colorSpace and transferFunction. Eight-bit samples below quality 100 are coded as photographs (a matrix/TRC profile through its own primaries and curves in XYB. A gray one, or one that needs a color engine, as the samples are) and are decoded back into the profile. Deeper samples use Modular coding. Gray needs identical RGB words. Profiles above 4 MiB select level 10. The complete profile and encoded output must fit available memory.

comes out

  • One record per requested output, shared by ordinary calls and queues. Only fit and variants can resolve without a file. A preserved JPEG has kept: 'jpeg-bytes', fidelity: 'exact' and measurements: []: the original JPEG restores exactly, with no claim of a measured score. Empty measurements add no independent verification claim. An encode can return meetsTarget: false with bytes when its measured look target was missed. An unmet size target (fit, variants) returns meetsTarget: false without a file.

  • format 'jxl' | 'jpeg' | 'png'

    The file's format: 'jxl', or 'jpeg' for a restored JPEG, or 'png'.
