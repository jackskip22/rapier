Rapier's factory brushes: with thanks to Brien Dieterle

Rapier's factory brushes grew from Brien Dieterle's brush pack for MyPaint (Dieterle-Brushes-v4,
https://github.com/briend/Brushes). Its nineteen presets ship with Rapier as MyPaint .myb files, and
Rapier's own Oil, Bristle, Flat, Scumble, Pencil, Pen and Smudge are derived from them. Feathers,
Tail feather and Tail plume are his.
