Draw's ornament packs (letter sets, templates and elements) are traced from the plates of Philipp Luidl and Helmut Huber,
"Ornamente / Ornaments" (Bruckmann, Munich, 1983; https://archive.org/details/luidl-p.-huber-h.-ornamente-ornaments).
The Archive item identifies its scan under Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International. That scan label
is separate from Rapier’s dedication of its own work below; the scanned edition is the third revised edition (1990).
The plates reproduce printed ornament by the artists, foundries and printers each caption names, in the years it gives
(the book's own pages and captions follow, German and English as printed). The selection, the traces and their arrangement
are Rapier's own and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

  scan 19 (printed page 18); three knots, two interlace panels and a strip.

  Academicism, about 1870, scan 164 (printed page 163). Independent frames, corner
  ornaments and rules separated from the printed specimen arrangement. The straight
  rules are geometric redraws; the long frame has three small local line repairs.
  Cartouche 22: Academicism, about 1870, scan 163; modern high-bodice adaptation.

  cut from type specimen pages, scans 94, 147 and 104.
