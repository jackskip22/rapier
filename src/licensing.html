Rapier licensing

The Rapier editor is free under AGPL-3.0-only, including for commercial use. Its Markdown and Will standards and the modules identified in LICENSE-MIT are available under MIT.

Alternative commercial licences are perpetual and cover one legal entity and its products, both editor builds and future public releases of the covered Rapier code. There are no per-product or seat charges, royalties, renewals, activation keys or version cutoffs.

The ordinary licence retains a modest “Includes Rapier” acknowledgement in About or legal notices. White-label removes that requirement. Third-party licence notices remain.

Support, custom work and hosted services are separate. A licence includes future releases if produced; it does not promise ongoing development.

Using Rapier in ChatGPT or Claude does not require a commercial licence. Hosted editing and review remain free.
