# Rapier

A place to write, draw and paint on your phone. Free, in your browser, with your work in your own files.

The founder’s aim: “everybody can make beautiful art for free on their budget android phone”.

## Make things

Write a document without learning Markdown, or edit its source directly. Add pictures, move and resize them, and wrap your words around their shape. Draw with a pen, brush, shapes and text, set the type the way a designer would (size, font, weight, slant, case, spacing, kerning) press a word into the paper like a letterpress, or set it in one of four alphabets of ornamental capitals traced from old books (each comes down the first time you choose it, then works offline), then drop a big letter in as the leading letter of a paragraph. Paint with watercolour, bristles, smudging and blending. Gather documents in Notes, with search, checklists, links, recordings and imports from other notes apps.

An agent can work with the document through Rapier’s tools and your Will: Intent, Add only or Lock. You do not need an agent to use Rapier.

## Your work stays yours

No Rapier account. No Rapier server needed to write, draw or save. The editor works offline; first installation and optional maths, diagram or PDF resources can need a download.

**Save keeps editable Markdown.** It is text any text editor can read, not a private database you need Rapier to unlock. Pictures can travel inside the same file. Enhanced layout and image formats may display differently in other Markdown readers.

**Share makes an offline web page**, with the editable source inside it. A page handed to somebody else never contacts a server, and neither does a standalone HTML export.

## Save a separate copy

Notes kept in a browser can be cleared by that browser. Back them up from the Notes settings, or sync with Cloudflare.

Use **BACKUP** in the Notes settings at the end of your session. Save the file to your device or another place you control, then check that it really arrived. A local save or an opened share sheet is not a separate backup. Without browser storage, notes can exist only until the page closes.

Every press of BACKUP makes a new file, named with its own date and time, so a later backup never replaces an earlier one and nothing is asked.

**Sync with Cloudflare**, in the Notes settings, keeps an encrypted copy of your notes in your own Cloudflare R2 bucket; Rapier never sees what is in it. Sync is not a backup: keep backing up too.

## Put it on your phone

Open `rapier.website` in your phone’s browser. Use the browser menu’s **Add to Home screen** or install command. Let the first load finish, then check it opens without a connection. You can also open `rapier.html` as a file and open it directly; do not assume that file-opened Notes has persistent storage.

*Rapier is in active development. The build receipt published with it (`docs/build.json` in the repository, `BUILD.json` on rapier.website) names the exact assembly of the file you are holding.*
