# Licensing

The Rapier editor is free under AGPL-3.0-only (LICENSE). The standard's own modules are MIT (LICENSE-MIT).

Alternative commercial licenses are paid once: USD 200 during 2026 (early adopter price; normally USD 1,000) with a visible “Editor by Rapier” credit, or USD 5,000 for white-label. Both cover one legal entity and its products, the whole editor (writing, Notes, Draw and painting), and future public releases of the covered Rapier code. There are no per-product, seat, installation or royalty charges, renewals, activation keys or version cutoffs.

The ordinary license requires the credit “Editor by Rapier”, linked to https://rapier.website, shown on the screen where people use the editor (the licensee chooses where on that screen; not only in a menu, an About page or legal notices). White-label requires no credit; third-party license notices remain. Support, custom work and hosted services are separate. Future releases are included if produced; ongoing development is not a service promised by the purchase.

The commercial agreement defines the alternative permissions. Prices include the license itself; applicable taxes are shown before payment. Public purchasing information is at https://rapier.website/commercial: the editor's own page opened at that address, with its checkout links written from `commercial-checkout.json` at build time. The editor has no button or menu entry for it beyond the Licenses sheet's "commercial licensing" line, and the hosted ChatGPT and Claude editor links only to informational licensing content.
